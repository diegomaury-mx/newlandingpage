# I scaled a national sustainability program and increased participation in the Southeast sevenfold

Key metric: +600% growth in participation in the Southeast (2019–2021). Documented baseline: 35 proposals from the region in the year prior to 2019. The final figure is our own operational estimate.

Role: National Program Director, Innovation & Entrepreneurship.

Demonstrated skills: program management · innovation program design · strategic partnerships · regional scaling.

Context: HEINEKEN Mexico and INCmty · 4 editions (2019–2022) · national reach, 6 months of operation per edition.

## Context

The HEINEKEN Green Challenge brings together, recognizes, and strengthens sustainable projects in Mexico. When I joined, the program already had national traction and a prize pool of 1,150,000 pesos, but the southeast was a dead zone: the entire region had submitted just 35 proposals the previous year. I’m from there.

The program didn’t have a problem with event execution. It had a systemic problem: recruitment depended on manual effort edition after edition, the application process wasn’t designed to scale, and regions outside the country’s center had no pathway to participate.

On top of that, in 2020 the entire program had to go virtual without missing its registration target.

> Thirty-five proposals from the entire Southeast isn’t a lack of talent. It’s a lack of access.

## Action

I was responsible for:

- The operation and execution of the program nationwide across all four editions.
- Designing the open innovation process and transitioning the application process to an online format.
- Building partnerships between the government, the private sector, universities, and the entrepreneurial ecosystem.
- Connecting participants with more than 100 mentors per edition.
- Logistical coordination for the six-month duration of each edition.

I was not responsible for:

- HEINEKEN’s brand budget or advertising investment.
- The final selection of winning projects.
- The company’s corporate sustainability strategy.

System built:

1. I redesigned the application process using Lean methodology and user experience mapping, and moved it online during the pandemic. It went from being a mere formality to a measurable funnel.
1. The national virtual bootcamp (2020–2022) was structured as a 5-day program, with 5 hours of daily activities per session.
1. I built the mentor network: more than 100 mentors per session, with assignment criteria based on the project’s stage and industry sector.
1. I forged partnerships with the government, universities, and the private sector to open regional entry points, rather than waiting for the national call for applications to reach the southeast on its own.
1. I designed REDUX as a pre-launch for the program: a bootcamp that fostered a culture of innovation before asking people to submit projects. The Tecnológico de Monterrey documented it as the largest of its kind in Latin America.

Trade-offs: I prioritized regional depth over gross national volume. Investing in partnerships in the southeast led to much higher growth in that region, but it meant not pursuing the maximum number of registrations that focusing on the center of the country would have yielded. It was the right decision for the impact goal, though not necessarily for the vanity metric.
