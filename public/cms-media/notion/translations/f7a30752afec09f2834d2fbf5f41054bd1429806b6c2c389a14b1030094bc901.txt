# What is HackSureste 2019?

As part of Universidad Modelo's "INGENIERÍA E INNOVACIÓN WEEKS 2019" program in Mérida, Yucatán, and in collaboration with CETIEM, we organized the second edition of HackSureste, which began as the idea of creating one of the go-to hackathons in Southeast Mexico. Bringing together students from 6 states across the country to take part. HackSureste's goal is to unlock human talent in technology, business, and marketing across the southeast of the country by bringing them together in healthy competition, skill-building, and applying talent through teamwork and mentorship from top experts. We believe in students' ability to create, and that's why we focus on motivating and supporting them.
