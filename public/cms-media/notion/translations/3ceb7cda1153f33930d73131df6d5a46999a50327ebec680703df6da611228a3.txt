For three years I led REDUX, a bootcamp to train entrepreneurs committed to sustainability that pivoted quickly to a virtual format during the pandemic, training 200+ people. Five editions ran (2020-2022) alongside INCmty. The methodology was included in Tecnológico de Monterrey's official program directory; the Director of Entrepreneurship recognized it as the youngest author's contribution in the directory. I built strategic partnerships with government, the private sector, and educational institutions, beating registration and impact targets every year. REDUX Agro is the edition focused on sustainable agriculture.
