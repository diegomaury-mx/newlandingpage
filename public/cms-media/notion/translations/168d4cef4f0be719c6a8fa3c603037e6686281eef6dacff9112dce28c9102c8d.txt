# I ran INCmty's open calls, and they grew 32% in a year

Anchor metric: 504 applicants to INC B-Challenge 2021, versus 383 in 2020. Tecnológico de Monterrey Annual Report 2021, p.415.

Role: National Program Director, Innovation & Entrepreneurship (2019-2022).

INCmty is the largest entrepreneurship festival in Latin America, and its open calls are the entry point to the ecosystem. I ran and operated all three (B-Challenge, Prototype, and Accelerator), designing each for a different stage of maturity: bootcamp for ideas, prototype for university students and researchers, acceleration for early-stage startups. Operations included partnerships with government, private sector, universities, and the ecosystem, plus project evaluation and selection.

Main result: all three open calls grew between 2020 and 2021. B-Challenge went from 383 to 504 applicants, Prototype from 156 to 197, and Accelerator from 152 to 205 startups; the 2021 Accelerator winner raised a one-million-dollar seed round. Caveat: applicant figures, not selected-participant figures. The seed round amount belongs to the winning startup, not attributable to the program as a return.
