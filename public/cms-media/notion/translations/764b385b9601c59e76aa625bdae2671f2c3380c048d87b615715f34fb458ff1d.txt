# I brought together 400 young people from 20 universities to discuss youth entrepreneurship

Anchor metric: 400 young entrepreneurs from 20 universities, May 1-3, 2015.

Role: general coordinator and executive producer.

The G20 YEA Model replicated the format of the G20's Young Entrepreneurs' Alliance to discuss youth entrepreneurship challenges and solutions. I coordinated the entire event: planning, executive production, designing the open innovation processes, community and registration management, and on-site facilitation. It drew all 20 universities of the Laguna Interuniversity Alliance.

Main result: 400 young business owners and entrepreneurs took part over three days. Caveat: attendance figure is self-reported, no publishable record. It's my first project at scale and the oldest one in the portfolio.
