# I scaled a national sustainability program and multiplied southeastern participation by 7

Anchor metric: +600% growth in southeastern participation (2019-2021). Documented baseline: 35 proposals from the region the year before 2019. The final figure is an own operational estimate.

Role: National Program Director, Innovation & Entrepreneurship.

Demonstrated capabilities: program leadership · innovation program design · strategic alliances · regional scaling.

Context: HEINEKEN México and INCmty · 4 editions (2019-2022) · national reach, 6 months of operation per edition.

## Context

The HEINEKEN Green Challenge brings together, recognizes, and strengthens sustainable projects in Mexico. When I joined, the program already had national traction and a 1,150,000-peso prize pool, but the southeast was dead territory: the entire region had submitted only 35 proposals the year before. That's where I'm from.

The program didn't have an event-execution problem. It had a systems problem: outreach depended on manual effort edition after edition, the application process wasn't built to scale, and regions outside the center of the country had no way in.

On top of that, in 2020 the entire program had to go virtual without losing sight of its registration targets.

> Thirty-five proposals from the entire southeast isn't a lack of talent. It's a lack of a door.

## Action

I was responsible for:

- Operating and executing the program nationally across all 4 editions.
- Designing the open innovation process and virtualizing the application process.
- Alliances between government, private sector, universities, and the entrepreneurial ecosystem.
- Connecting participants with more than 100 mentors per edition.
- Coordinating the logistics of each edition's six-month run.

I was not responsible for:

- HEINEKEN's brand budget or advertising investment.
- The final selection of winning projects.
- The company's corporate sustainability strategy.

System built:

1. I redesigned the application process using Lean methodology and user experience mapping, and virtualized it during the pandemic. It stopped being paperwork and became a measurable funnel.
1. The national virtual bootcamp (2020-2022) was structured as a 5-day program, with 5 hours of activities per day per edition.
1. I built the mentor network: more than 100 per edition, with assignment criteria based on project stage and vertical.
1. I built alliances with government, universities, and the private sector to open regional entry points, instead of waiting for the national call to reach the southeast on its own.
1. I designed REDUX as a pre-launch for the program: a bootcamp that built an innovation culture before asking people for projects. Tecnológico de Monterrey documented it as the largest of its kind in Latin America.

Trade-offs: I prioritized regional depth over raw national volume. Investing in southeastern alliances produced far higher growth in that region, but it meant not chasing the maximum absolute registrations that focusing on the center of the country would have delivered. It was the right call for the impact goal, not necessarily for the vanity metric.
