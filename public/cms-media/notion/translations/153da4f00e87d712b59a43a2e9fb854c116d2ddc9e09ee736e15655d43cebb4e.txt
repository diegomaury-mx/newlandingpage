> Event Details, Archive Section. The actual source for this event is the HEINEKEN Green Challenge. This section only contains specific details about the 2020 edition.

## Context of the Edition

Central theme: circular economy. This was the first edition held entirely online due to the pandemic, with the national bootcamp (5 days, 5 hours per day) serving as a pre-launch for the program.

## Diego’s Role

National Program Director, Innovation & Entrepreneurship. National program operations: call for entries, matching with mentors, partnerships with the government, the private sector, and universities, and logistics for all stages throughout the six-month program.

## Verified Figures

- 3,975 entrepreneurs
- 1,325 projects
- 562 universities
- +214% growth compared to the previous edition
