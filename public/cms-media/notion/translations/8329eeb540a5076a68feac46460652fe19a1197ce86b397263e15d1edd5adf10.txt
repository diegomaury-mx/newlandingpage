## What is HackSureste Industria 4.0?

HackSureste started as the idea of creating one of southeastern Mexico's leading hackathons. Bringing together students from 6 states of the country to participate. HackSureste's goal is to boost human talent in technology, business, and marketing across the southeast of the country by bringing them into healthy competition, skill development, and applying talent through teamwork and mentorship from top experts. We believe in students' capacity to create, so we focus on motivating and supporting them.

## Allies and Sponsors

> Key facts (update)

- First HackSureste edition: October 27-28, 2018, in Mérida, with close to 250 participants.
- Founder and Director: Diego Maury (sole founder).
- Coverage: southeastern Mexico (Veracruz, Tabasco, Campeche, Yucatán, and Quintana Roo).
- HackSureste's cumulative impact: 3,000+ participants, 4 locations, 100+ startups, and 30+ programs.
- Main historical sponsors: IBM, Ictansoft, CCI, Cardinal, Grupo Bepensa, Interact Soluciones, Grupo Gamma, and National Soft.
