For three years I led REDUX, a training program for sustainable entrepreneurs that adapted to virtual delivery during the pandemic, training 200+ people. Five editions took place (2020-2022) in collaboration with INCmty. The methodology was included in Tecnológico de Monterrey's official directory; the director of Entrepreneurship recognized it as a contribution from the youngest author in the directory. Strategic alliances were established between government, private sector, and educational institutions, beating registration and impact targets every year. REDUX Energy is the edition focused on energy efficiency.
