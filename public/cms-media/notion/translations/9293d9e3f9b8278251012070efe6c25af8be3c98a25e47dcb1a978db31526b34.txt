# I led the INCmty call for proposals, and applications grew by 32% in one year

Key metric: 504 applicants for the 2021 INC B-Challenge, compared to 383 in 2020. Tecnológico de Monterrey 2021 Annual Report, p. 415.

Role: National Program Director, Innovation & Entrepreneurship (2019–2022).

INCmty is the largest entrepreneurship festival in Latin America, and its calls for entries serve as the gateway to the ecosystem. I led and managed all three programs (B-Challenge, Prototype, and Accelerator), designing each for a different stage of maturity: a bootcamp for ideas, a prototype track for college students and researchers, and an accelerator for early-stage startups. The operation involved partnerships with the government, the private sector, universities, and the startup ecosystem, as well as the evaluation and selection of projects.

Key result: All three programs saw growth between 2020 and 2021. B-Challenge grew from 383 to 504 applicants, Prototype from 156 to 197, and Accelerator from 152 to 205 startups; the winner of the 2021 Accelerator received a seed round of one million dollars. Caveat: These figures represent applicants, not selected participants. The amount of the seed round refers to the winner and is not attributable to the program as a return.
