### Key results and actions in REDUX:
1. Development and execution: created and designed the REDUX bootcamp from scratch for sustainable entrepreneurs, aligned with the SDGs.
2. Adaptation to virtual format: methodology combining asynchronous activities and structured connection for remote learning during the pandemic.
3. Strategic partnerships: collaborations with the public sector, private sector, academia, and the entrepreneurial ecosystem.
4. Recognition: the methodology was included in Tecnológico de Monterrey's official program directory; the Director of Entrepreneurship recognized it as a contribution from the directory's youngest author.
5. Training: 200+ people trained throughout the program.
6. Impact: five editions (2020-2022) covering circular economy, sustainable agriculture, and energy efficiency, exceeding the annual registration target every year.
7. Activation of local ecosystems focused on sustainability.
