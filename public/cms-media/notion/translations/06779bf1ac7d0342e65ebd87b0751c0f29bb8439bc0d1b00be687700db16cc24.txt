## Context

In 2015 I joined the Young Entrepreneurs Commission of COPARMEX Laguna as VP of Outreach. The commission had volunteers and reach, but no structural channel to universities: the business sector and student communities operated disconnected, and every outreach effort depended on personal relationships, not a system.

## Action

I designed and operated a university chapter model with standardized operating rules and multi-level mutual-benefit agreements between university presidents, students, and business owners, replicable across cities.

- Laguna (2015-2016, Outreach): first outreach with 10 institutions (17 elements across mentions, agreements, and faculties) and integration events like The Gravity Rally Games, with 100 students and partners like Grupo Lala and Club Santos.
- Mérida (2016-2019, Projects and University Chapters; 2019-2022, Entrepreneurial Development and Innovation): expanded the model to 8 more institutions (15 elements), with agreements, inauguration ceremonies, and a measurable activation rule: a chapter only counted as functional once it activated its community with events, calls to action, or joint programs.
- Public-impact campaigns: the Yo No Doy Mordida anti-corruption initiative (decree signed on March 11, 2017 at the Remate de Paseo Montejo), the #ParticipoVotoExijo university tour (2017-2018), and the #MXconFuturo Forum (2018).

## Result

| --- | --- | --- |
| University chapters and outreach | 0 (2015) | 32 elements across 18 institutions in La Laguna and Yucatán (2022) |

The outreach work in Mérida, including a networking event alongside INCmty, put my name on the ecosystem's radar and led to my next chapter: national director of the HEINEKEN Green Challenge.
