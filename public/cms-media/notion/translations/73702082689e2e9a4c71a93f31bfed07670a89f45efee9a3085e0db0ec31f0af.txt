For four years, I held the role of National Program Director, Innovation & Entrepreneurship (call design and operation) for entrepreneurship at INCmty, in partnership with Tecnológico de Monterrey. I designed and ran calls for different entrepreneurship stages, built strategic partnerships, and generated valuable content to strengthen the entrepreneurship ecosystem. I implemented programs that offered benefits, funding, and opportunities to participants. Responsibilities included facilitation, general coordination, process design, community management, speaking engagements, and mentoring. Through these actions, we exceeded our annual registration and impact targets.

INC Prototype calls on innovators with prototypes at TRL levels 3 through 8, sorting projects into two categories:
1. TRL 3 to 5: early- to mid-stage prototypes.
2. TRL 6 to 8: advanced prototypes with high market potential.

Finalists are selected to present to experts, and in-kind prizes are awarded to the top projects in each category.
