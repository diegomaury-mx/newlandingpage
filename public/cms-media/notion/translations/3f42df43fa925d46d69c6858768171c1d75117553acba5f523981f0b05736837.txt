# Make It Happen: 49 posts and an uncomfortable lesson about distribution

Anchor metric: 49 posts. My brand's main editorial vehicle.

Role: author and editor.

Haz que pase (Make It Happen) is my newsletter on Substack, and today it's the channel where I write what I think without intermediaries. It's the only project in the portfolio where I am the product.

Main result: 49 posts. Most traffic arrives direct, but converts very little to subscription. Email campaigns attract users and don't convert. Social channels (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram) bring volume and almost no subscriptions. The only channel with a decent conversion rate is Substack itself, which brings the least traffic. Caveat: platform metrics, no absolute subscriber counts recorded in this file.
