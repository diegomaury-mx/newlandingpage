# ideaLab: I extended HackSureste into sustainable projects

Anchor metric: not recorded.

Role: co-founder.

ideaLab isn't an independent organization: it's a program derived from HackSureste, focused on fostering innovation in sustainable projects. A hackathon produces ideas in 48 hours and abandons them; ideaLab existed for what happens after. It accompanied participants in developing and validating their ideas with training, workshops, mentorships, and access to industry experts.
