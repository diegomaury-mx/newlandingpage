While designing and building SOFI, I discovered that the real bottleneck wasn’t a lack of staff but rather the absence of a coherent operational workflow; so I began by mapping every touchpoint and defining clear prioritization criteria before choosing the technology, which allowed me to quickly integrate channels and CRM without relying on premature decisions about voice or text. Each iteration revealed that user trust was eroded when the interaction was too automated, which forced me to reevaluate the channel strategy and revert to WhatsApp, demonstrating that the technological solution must yield to customer behavior. Furthermore, by centralizing information in Notion and HubSpot, I learned that data governance and ongoing documentation are just as critical as AI architecture, since without them the system loses stability and scalability. Ultimately, the experience taught me that a successful project combines a strategic vision with the flexibility to adapt tools to the human realities that arise along the way.
