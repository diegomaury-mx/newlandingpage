The HEINEKEN Green Challenge is an initiative driven by HEINEKEN México and INCmty that seeks to bring together, recognize, and strengthen the country's most relevant sustainable projects. For 5 years, I was responsible for the Heineken Green Challenge's operations and execution. Across the country, I handled relationship-building, planning, and outreach with participants, more than 100 mentors, and coordinated logistics for every stage of this project, which ran for 6 months each year. I also coordinated strategic partnerships between government, the private sector, the entrepreneurial ecosystem, and universities, hitting the brand's registration and impact target every year. I also designed and implemented entrepreneurial skill-building for participants and everyone involved at the project's different stages. I successfully ran the project's operations despite the administrative challenges that came up year after year. Over the course of my involvement, we reached close to 3,000 entrepreneurs across different sustainability areas and stages of growth. Roles held: Facilitator, General Coordinator, Open Innovation Process Designer, Community and Registration Manager, Speaker, and Mentor.

This year the HEINEKEN Green Challenge 2022 theme is focused on energy efficiency, so we're looking for great ideas or innovative ventures related to this topic. If you're someone who wants to make a positive impact on the environment, this is your chance!
