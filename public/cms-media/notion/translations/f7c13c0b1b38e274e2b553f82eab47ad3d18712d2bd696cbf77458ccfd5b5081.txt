I published the full curriculum and never signed it as a product. Today the methodology circulates, people use it, and there's no way to track that use or bring the authorship back to me: I gave away the asset without keeping the brand. The lesson isn't "don't publish in the open." It's that opening the content and capitalizing on authorship aren't incompatible, and I did the first without doing the second.

Transferable: when the program is the methodology, the asset isn't the event, it's the curriculum. Treat it as a product from day one.
