Key results and actions:

1. Brought together 400 young entrepreneurs: convened 400 students from 20 different universities to discuss youth entrepreneurship challenges.

2. Digital registration system: implemented a registration system through Eventbrite to efficiently manage sign-ups.

3. Attendance tracking: ran manual attendance tracking during the event to ensure active participation.

4. Organized workshops and talks: ran workshops, training sessions, and talks, both in-person and online, using live-streaming technology.

5. Logistics and coordination: as general coordinator, planned and executed every activity, juggling multiple roles including facilitator, executive producer, and mentor.

6. Effective communication strategy: promoted the event door-to-door at each university, filling the event despite outreach challenges.

7. Overcame challenges: managed the varied interests of stakeholders involved and secured a suitable venue for the event, contributing to its success.

8. Impact on youth entrepreneurship: the event set an important precedent in youth entrepreneurship at the international level.
