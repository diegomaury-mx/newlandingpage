Being part of COPARMEX was truly important to me. I’ve always said this: one of my schools of life was the Tecnológico de Monterrey, and another was COPARMEX.

COPARMEX definitely taught me not only what it means to start a business, but also what it takes to be a responsible entrepreneur: to understand that an entrepreneur’s commitment to their community never ceases to exist or to grow.

Having the opportunity to serve as vice president of Jóvenes COPARMEX for more than five years was an honor and a pleasure. It truly left a lasting mark on the way I view business and recognize the importance of the country’s economic development without sacrificing social well-being.

It really was an incredible experience.
