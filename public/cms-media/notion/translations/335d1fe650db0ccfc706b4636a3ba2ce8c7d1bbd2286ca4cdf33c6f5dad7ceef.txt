The project grew into something much bigger than myself. I was afraid to let go and bring more people on board, and I ended up limiting its development. I had a hard time giving it the space to grow beyond what I could handle.

I don’t regret what happened. I enjoyed it immensely, and I’m happy with the result. I’m also left with a nagging feeling that I could have done better—that I could have trusted others more and shared the responsibility.

I can be proud of what we built and acknowledge where I fell short. Today I understand that part of taking care of a project is also learning to let it go.
