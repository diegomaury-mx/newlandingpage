Key results and actions:

1. Workshop organization: Ran the BTEM Training workshop, formerly known as STARTUP Training, aimed at students interested in Tecnológico de Monterrey's Entrepreneurial Talent Scholarship.

2. Full support: Provided personalized guidance and advising to participants, helping them consolidate their entrepreneurial ideas.

3. Resources and tools: Provided resources and tools to strengthen participants' business ideas, increasing their odds of success in the scholarship application.

4. Promoting entrepreneurial talent: Promoted innovation and entrepreneurship in the community, recognizing entrepreneurial talent at the national and international level.

5. Partnership facilitation: Managed strategic partnerships and promoted the call for applications, creating high-value content for participants.

6. Community impact: Contributed to the growth and development of the entrepreneurial community, encouraging active participation from academics and entrepreneurs in competitions.
