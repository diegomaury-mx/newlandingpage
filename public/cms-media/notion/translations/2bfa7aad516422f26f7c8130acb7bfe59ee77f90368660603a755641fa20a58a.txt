### Key results and actions
1. HackSureste, founded in 2018, drives technology innovation and entrepreneurship in Mexico.
2. Managed open innovation projects and industry collaborations.
3. Events run in partnership with universities, positioning HackSureste as one of the leading hackathons in southeastern Mexico (editions in Mérida and Ciudad del Carmen).
4. HackSureste WIRE (an extension of HackSureste, with RushFrame): free online talks and workshops on entrepreneurship, technology, and innovation.
