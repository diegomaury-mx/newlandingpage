# I turned inefficient lead capture into a business operation that runs on its own.

Lead capture went from taking 1 to 3 days to under 5 minutes. That's the change at the heart of this case.

Capabilities demonstrated: RevOps and automation · AI-powered systems · Governance and processes · Program leadership.

Context: PropTech (confidential) · Oct 2025 - Jun 2026 · fractional operations leadership covering strategy, RevOps, marketing, automation, and AI.

---

## The problem

It wasn't a lack of hands. It was a lack of a system.

Leads were coming in through multiple channels, Meta Lead Ads among them, with nothing and no one to receive them. A lead would arrive and no one would touch it for one to three days. By the time someone responded, the prospect was either completely cold or had already gone to a competitor.

There was no system to automatically assign each lead. There were no clear criteria to decide who to attend to first. The CRM had no single source of truth: each area worked with its own version of the data.

> You don't fix that by hiring someone to respond faster. You fix it by building the system.

## The intervention

Four decisions changed the operation completely.

### Decision 01: Make every lead land in the right place

I connected the capture channels to HubSpot so every lead entered the sales process automatically, without depending on someone checking an inbox, using scenarios built in Make.com (formerly Integromat)

Meta Lead Ads · Make · HubSpot

### Decision 02: Put the data in order

I organized HubSpot so everyone worked with the same information and the same rules. I defined the stages, the criteria for prioritizing leads, and the information that needed to be logged at each step.

HubSpot · lifecycle · lead scoring · pipeline

### Decision 03: Make first response stop depending on a person

I built SOFI to receive and qualify leads automatically. That way, first response stopped depending on whether someone happened to be available at that moment.

The system combines AI for conversation, reasoning, and transcription.

### Decision 04: Leave the system documented

I documented how the operation works and centralized the information in one place. Processes, reports, decisions, and follow-up stopped depending on information scattered across people and tools.

Notion · processes · dashboards · reporting

## The decision that changed the system

### Technology had to give way to behavior.

SOFI was born as a voice agent. The technology worked. But we discovered something more important: prospects didn't answer calls from unknown numbers, but they did use WhatsApp.

I changed the primary channel and rebuilt the necessary part of the system to adapt it. The AI logic stayed the same.

> The channel is a business hypothesis. Not an architecture decision.
