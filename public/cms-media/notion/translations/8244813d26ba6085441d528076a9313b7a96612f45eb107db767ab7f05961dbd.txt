Over a three-year period, I had the honor of leading REDUX, an innovative bootcamp designed specifically to train entrepreneurs committed to sustainability. This exciting project, which I conceived entirely on my own, stood out for how quickly it adapted to a virtual format during the difficult pandemic situation, achieving highly productive results for all participants.

Across five successful consecutive editions, REDUX ran in close collaboration with Inc Monterrey, and its main goal was to train a new generation of entrepreneurs passionate about environmental sustainability. The innovative methodology I developed for this bootcamp was recognized and officially included in the program and call-for-applications directory of the prestigious Tecnológico de Monterrey, which made me the youngest author to be part of that directory.

I also managed to build successful strategic partnerships between government, the private sector, the entrepreneurial ecosystem, and educational institutions during my tenure. These valuable collaborations let us beat our registration and impact targets year after year under the well-known REDUX brand. As a result, we reached new levels of success and recognition in our mission to train and empower the entrepreneurs of the future.
