For 5 years, I was responsible for the Heineken Green Challenge's operations and execution. Across the country, I built relationships, planning, and partnerships with participants, more than 100 mentors, and coordinated the logistics of every stage of this project, which ran for 6 months each year.

I also coordinated strategic alliances between government, the private sector, the entrepreneurship ecosystem, and universities, hitting the brand's registration and impact goals every year. I also designed and implemented entrepreneurial skill-building for participants and everyone involved across the project's stages. I successfully ran the project's operations despite the administrative challenges that came up year after year.

Over my 5 years of involvement, we reached close to 3,000 entrepreneurs across different sustainability areas and stages of growth. Roles held: Facilitator, General Coordinator, Open Innovation Process Designer, Community and Registration Manager, Speaker, and Mentor.
