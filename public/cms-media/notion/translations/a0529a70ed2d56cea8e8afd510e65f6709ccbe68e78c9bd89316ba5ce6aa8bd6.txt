# INC Accelerator: 205 startups competing, and the winner raised a million dollars

Anchor metric: 205 applicant startups in 2021, versus 152 in 2020. The 2021 winner received a $1 million seed round. Tec's 2021 Annual Report, p.415.

Role: National Program Director, Innovation & Entrepreneurship.

INC Accelerator is the call for applications at the mature end of INCmty's funnel: early-stage startups from across Latin America, not ideas. I directed and ran the call for applications, the strategic partnerships, and the evaluation and selection process for projects entering the acceleration program.

Main result: the call for applications grew from 152 applicant startups in 2020 to 205 in 2021, a 35% increase, and that edition's winner closed a $1 million seed investment round. Caveat: the round is an achievement of the winning startup, not a return attributable to the program or to me. I cite it as a signal of pipeline quality, not as my own result.
