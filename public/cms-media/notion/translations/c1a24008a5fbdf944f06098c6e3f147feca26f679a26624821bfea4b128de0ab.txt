### Key results and actions
1. HackSureste, founded in 2018, an organization dedicated to technology innovation and entrepreneurship in Mexico.
2. Management of open innovation projects and industry partnerships.
3. Events in collaboration with universities; HackSureste established itself as one of the reference hackathons of the southeast (Mérida and Ciudad del Carmen).
4. Partnerships with government, private sector, and universities.
5. ideaLab (HackSureste extension): program to foster innovation in sustainable projects, with training, workshops, mentorships, and networking.
6. Promotion of sustainable entrepreneurship and responsible environmental practices.
